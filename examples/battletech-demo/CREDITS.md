# Credits and provenance

The port's reverse-engineering credits and AI disclosure are in the repository
[README](../../README.md#acknowledgements). This scenario and its integration
were developed with AI assistance, continuing the project's work with OpenAI
Sol 6.1 and Astra. No original game source code is included or used as port code.

The nine mech variants are a subset of the existing
[Updated roster](../../content/updated/README.md), adapted from records maintained
by the **MegaMek mm-data contributors**. `sources.json` retains eight pinned source
links/hashes and the initial pack's unpinned Panther URL (no original source hash
was recorded for that entry). `SOURCE-README.md` and `ART-SOURCE-README.md` preserve
the source pack's attribution when exporting this package. Their relative links
refer to the source repository layout. The numeric adapter preserves the pack's armor,
structure, movement, heat sinks, mounted weapons and ammunition. Display names
are expanded from the short labels. Prices remain authored CHI-scale prices;
weapon ranges, damage resolution and rear-weapon behavior use the documented
port adaptations. This is not a claim of complete tabletop equivalence.

The ten vehicle presets come from the repository's editable vehicle catalogue.
[VEHICLE-SOURCE-README.md](VEHICLE-SOURCE-README.md) preserves the Sarna contributor
attribution, individual design links, factual/adapted-stat distinctions and
vehicle-rule limitations. It is copied with the package by New project and
Export. Its repository-relative links refer to the source tree layout.
Their 32-image driving sprites were independently drawn with AI assistance;
no Sarna artwork or original CHI vehicle sprites were copied.

The three ANM portraits are copies of this repository's previously AI-generated
broker, training and ComStar art. Their prompts and production provenance are in
[the art README](../../content/updated/art/README.md); no portrait was extracted
from the original game. Original map tiles, fonts, mech proxy sprites, borders and combat
sound are loaded at runtime from the original files/caches supplied by the player.

The broad setting follows the official descriptions of the
[Lyran Commonwealth](https://battletech.com/factions/lyran-commonwealth/) and
[mercenary commands](https://battletech.com/factions/mercenary/), consulted
2026-10-17. Grayhaven, Beacon Company, its pilots and the contracts are original
fan fiction. The local relay is deliberately described as a local communications
station, not an HPG installation.
