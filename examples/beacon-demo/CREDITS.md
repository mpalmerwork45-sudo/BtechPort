# Credits and provenance

Last Beacon was authored for BtechPort with AI-assisted implementation. Its
characters, setting, quest text and Workshop walker statistics are invented.
Pixels and short tone sequences are drawn/generated by our own
`tools/make_beacon_demo.py`. The five-by-seven glyphs come from BtechPort's
independently drawn diagnostic font, rather than a BIOS or DOS-game font.

The reusable tactical engine follows BtechPort's documented research and
retains its GPL-3.0-only licensing and contributor acknowledgements. No original
game source code is used. See the main README for the contributions of the
UnBattletech, Reko-Btech, CrescentHawksTools and map-research contributors and
the disclosure of AI use with Sol 6.1 and Astra.

The independently authored example does not incorporate the Updated pack's
adapted external mech specifications or its separately licensed illustrations.
The example's assets are GPL-3.0-only. Lua is MIT licensed, with the unchanged
notice and official-release checksum in `third_party/lua/`.
